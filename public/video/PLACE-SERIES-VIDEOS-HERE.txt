Drop per-series background videos here, named by series number:

    series-1.mp4
    series-2.mp4
    ...

The homepage plays /video/series-N.mp4 as a muted, looping background behind the
node board on desktop (screens wider than 800px), for whichever series' case is
selected. If the file is missing, the homepage just shows the plain dark background
(it fails gracefully) — so adding series-1.mp4 here is all it takes to light it up.

Use a dark, low-motion, web-optimised MP4 (H.264). This .txt is only here to keep
the folder in the repo; you can delete it once real videos are added.
