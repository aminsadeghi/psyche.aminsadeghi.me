**Attachment → Jealousy → Envy → Resentment**

Jealousy has to be kept apart from Envy, because readers mix them up:

- **Jealousy** has **three people**. It is fear of **losing what you hold** to a rival.
- **Envy** has **two people**. It is pain at **what someone else has**.

The ground is already laid. *Fear* (I.3) prints: "Jealousy is the same fear turned towards losing what you
already hold." *Overinvestment* (IV.13) calls the grip "jealousy's cousin". The Jealousy case cashes both.

## The Psyche case: sketch

**Title:** *Jealousy*. Page 2 may carry *Manufactured Jealousy* as its own title.

1. **The fear of loss.** Jealousy is fear pointed at losing what you hold. The tell is **rigidity** (picked up
   from *Fear*): checking, scanning, needing to know. Draw the three-person geometry, and set it against Envy.
2. **Manufactured Jealousy.** Some people **provoke** jealousy on purpose. Research names it
   *jealousy induction* (leads below). Its motives are to test the bond, to punish, to win back control, and
   to borrow worth from being wanted. **Ambiguity is the weapon:** show just enough (a second shadow, a
   second glass) to make the other person ask "who are you with?"
3. **The broadcast.** Stories and songs are **proxy voices**. One track plays the **martyr** ("I gave
   everything, you took it"). Another plays the **phantom suitor** ("someone else wants to know my status").
   They contradict each other, and the contradiction is the point: guilt and fear of loss in one evening.
   **The contradiction is the panic.** Add the **horizontal run** here: the trip goes on because
   going home means facing the burned bridge.
4. **The audience.** This page is for the person watching. Do not try to solve the contradiction. The
   performance needs a reply to work. Silence is not punishment; it is declining a role. End on the empty
   theatre.

**House rules that apply (from `FEAR.md`):** write it as a **lens, not a fact**. Say *"one reason people
post like this is…"* and never *"she is doing X"*. GeminiPro reads intent off a few stories with total
certainty ("a calculated weapon"). Nobody can know that from a screen. The case should name the
**honest alternative**: sometimes a song is only a mood, and a trip is only a trip. The **pattern** shows in
the repetition, not in any single post. That is the stronger page anyway, because the reader's own
uncertainty is exactly what manufactured jealousy feeds on.

**Copyright:** song lyrics are **never quoted** on any of the sites. Name the song, or describe it.

### Research leads (to verify before printing)

- **Jealousy induction scale and motives.** A Romantic Relationship Jealousy-Induction Scale with a
  motives measure. It is linked to attachment anxiety and avoidance, lower commitment, and
  game-playing ([ProQuest](https://www.proquest.com/docview/1032601079)). *Check the authors and year;
  probably Mattingly et al. 2012, Current Psychology.*
- **Induction and partner violence.** Toledo Adolescent Relationships Study, 892 young adults. Inducing
  jealousy is associated with verbal conflict and with partner violence. The data are correlational
  ([OJP](https://www.ojp.gov/ncjrs/virtual-library/abstracts/inducing-jealousy-and-intimate-partner-violence-among-young-adults)).
- **Social-media jealousy.** A 2024 systematic review of 45 studies
  ([Emerald, *Internet Research*](https://emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/INTR-02-2020-0103/full/pdf)).
  The field starts with Muise et al. 2009 (Facebook jealousy).
- **Classic:** White (1980), induced jealousy as a power move. *Verify.*
- **Song 1:** Sade, *No Ordinary Love* (1992). Confirmed. Note that Sade is also a stock "romantic
  getaway" mood track, which is the honest alternative reading.
- **Song 2:** attributed by GeminiPro to Dame Atlas, *dame's interlude*. **Unverified.** A search found
  no track or lyric match. Don't print the attribution until it is confirmed.

she is not alone (it's unclear whether the companion is a man or a woman, and it might be a romantic

   another city keeps the "green screen" on.

   silence and ask who she is with.
4. **The contradiction of the soundtrack:** the martyr script (guilt) and the phantom-suitor script (fear

5. **Advice:** don't solve the contradiction. The contradiction is the panic. Let her perform to an empty
   theatre.
