# The Psyche — vision & scope

**Site:** psyche.aminsadeghi.me · **World:** Inner World · **Status:** fresh, awaiting first case

## What it is

The **modern** voice of the Inner World: psychology — the architecture of the self,
self-esteem, how a person is formed, wounded, and made whole. Where **The Forge**
(planned) goes *back* through Jung to hermetic philosophy, **The Psyche** stays in the
present: today's psychology, read as story rather than measured as data.

The name is exact: *psychology* is literally *psyche* + *logos* — the ancient Greek
word for soul, wearing a modern lab coat. It rhymes with its Greek-rooted siblings,
*Mythos* and (its deep-time counterpart) the Forge. See the collection map in
[PROJECT-SCOPE.md](https://aminsadeghi.me/PROJECT-SCOPE.md).

## Form

Same case-file format as The Archives — but here "case" carries its clinical second
meaning: a **case study** of a mind. Each piece is a series of short pages that read the
psychology as narrative.
